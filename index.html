Jan Petrš

Researcher in Robotics and Architecture | EPFL

I envision a future where buildings self-configure from intelligent components, enabling construction to become more sustainable, optimized, and authentic. Spaces will dynamically adapt based on their use. Originally trained as an architect, I now focus on robotics at EPFL—particularly walkable tensegrities—blending design and research at the intersection of robotics, materials, and the built environment.
